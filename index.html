About the dates

The occasions below use commonly observed Twelver Shia Hijri dates. Gregorian dates are calculated automatically. Actual observance can differ by one day depending on moon sighting, marjaʿ, and local religious authority.

Included occasions

Births and martyrdoms of the Ahl al-Bayt, Ashura and Arbaeen, Laylat al-Qadr, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Ghadir, Mab'ath, Mubahala, Islamic New Year and other major Shia commemorations.

For religious scheduling, verify dates with your local Shia Islamic center or marjaʿ. This calendar is intended as a website-friendly reference, not a moon-sighting authority.
